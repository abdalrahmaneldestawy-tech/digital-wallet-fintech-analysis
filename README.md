## 📌 Executive Summary
This project delivers a comprehensive **FinTech Analytics Dashboard** designed to evaluate customer lifetime value (LTV), transaction dynamics, user retention, and customer satisfaction across **7,000 active digital wallet users**. 

The goal of this analysis is to empower growth, marketing, and customer care teams with actionable insights to optimize user retention and drive revenue growth.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
* **Total Customers:** 7,000 Unique Digital Wallet Users
* **Total Volume Spent:** $35.01 Billion
* **Total Transactions:** 3.51 Million
* **Average Customer LTV:** $511.9K
* **Average CSAT Score:** 5.48 / 10

---

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 1. Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) Distribution
* **Middle-Income** and **Suburban/Rural** users show the highest long-term financial value (~$0.53M Avg LTV).
* High-income urban users represent a strong potential for high-ticket transactions but require tailored retention strategies.

### 2. User Inactivity & Retention Risks
* Users with low CSAT scores exhibit an average inactivity period of **~184 days** (~6 months).
* **Action Item:** Proactive support intervention is critical for users reaching 90+ days of inactivity to mitigate customer churn.

### 3. Payment Method Preferences
* Transaction volumes are evenly distributed across **UPI (25.59%)**, **Credit Cards (25.16%)**, **Wallet Balance (24.8%)**, and **Debit Cards (24.46%)**, highlighting a well-balanced ecosystem for payment gateways.

### 4. Rewards & Incentive Effectiveness
* Cashback distribution is concentrated heavily among Middle-Income users (~$6.0M total), directly correlating with higher transaction frequency.
